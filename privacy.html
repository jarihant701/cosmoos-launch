Privacy notice

Last updated 24 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to your personal data when you visit cosmoos.tech or join the CosmoOS early-access waitlist. CosmoOS is in a closed pre-launch stage, and the only personal data we ask for is your email address.

Who we are

CosmoOS (“we”, “us”) is responsible for your data, as the data fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and as the controller under the GDPR where it applies. For anything about your data, email hello@cosmoos.tech. The same address is our contact for grievances.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you submit the waitlist form.
  • Signup details recorded by our email provider with your signup: the date and time, which form you used, and whether you confirmed your address, opened our emails or clicked their links.
  • Technical data that any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type. Our hosting and form providers use it to deliver pages, keep them secure and count how often the form is viewed and submitted.

We do not ask for your name, phone number or payment details. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The embedded signup form saves a timestamp in your browser’s local storage so that a repeat visit is not counted as a new form view. It does not identify you.

Why we use it

We use your email address only to:

  • tell you that you are on the waitlist;
  • invite you when early access opens; and
  • send occasional updates about the CosmoOS launch.

We rely on your consent, which you give by submitting the form. We do not sell your data, rent it or share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Who handles it for us

We use these service providers, who process data on our instructions:

  • Hostinger (Hostinger Reach) hosts the signup form, stores the waitlist and sends our emails.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts this website.

These providers may process data on servers outside India, including in the European Union and the United States. We use them only for the purposes above.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until 12 months after CosmoOS opens to the public, whichever comes first. If you create a CosmoOS account, the app’s own privacy terms apply to that account.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • unsubscribe, using the link at the bottom of any email we send;
  • withdraw your consent, which stops future emails;
  • ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapacitated; and
  • raise a grievance with us.

Email hello@cosmoos.tech and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The waitlist is for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the address.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Access to the waitlist is limited to the CosmoOS team, and the account that holds it is protected by our provider’s sign-in controls.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email before it applies.
