One brand deal, from brief to paid

Every collaboration becomes a project. Its brief, deliverables, content, feedback and invoice stay connected, so nothing has to be pieced together from chats and spreadsheets.

  1. Add the brand

    Keep each brand or client you work with, with their projects in one place.

  2. Upload the brief

    AI pulls out deliverables, deadlines, payment terms and usage rights, each with a quote from the document. You correct it and approve one version as the baseline.

  3. Plan and create

    Schedule posts in a list, calendar or board. Draft hooks, scripts and captions in AI Studio and save the keepers to your plan.

  4. Check the feedback

    Paste client feedback to compare it with the approved brief, spot possible scope creep, and record revision rounds against the agreed limit.

  5. Get paid

    Record invoices per project and see what is owed, overdue and received, in each currency, without mixing them.

Everything your creator business orbits around

Four connected parts of one workspace, built for creators who work with brands.

Plan
Ideas, drafts and posting dates for Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn and more, in a list, calendar or board.
Create
AI Studio drafts content ideas, hooks, scripts, captions and hashtags, and repurposes content for another platform. Every draft is yours to edit.
Manage
Brands, projects, briefs, deliverables, feedback and revision history, linked so each deal keeps its own record.
Grow
A dashboard of what needs attention: deadlines, open deliverables, and invoices owed, overdue and paid.

You decide what ships

  • Nothing posts or sends itself

    CosmoOS never publishes to your platforms or messages a client. You post and reply; it keeps the record.

  • AI drafts, you approve

    AI output stays a draft until you edit and save it, and AI never changes a brief, deliverable or revision limit.

  • Your brief is the baseline

    Approved brief versions are locked and kept as history, so what was agreed cannot quietly drift.

  • Private by default

    Each workspace is isolated. Briefs and client feedback are treated as data, never as instructions, and are sent to AI only with privacy-first routing when you choose to analyse them.

Questions

Can I use CosmoOS today?

Not publicly yet. CosmoOS is preparing a closed beta. Join the waitlist and you will get an invitation when early access opens. Meanwhile, try the interactive demo: a sample brand deal with sample data, not the full product. No sign-up, and nothing is saved.

Who is it for?

Creators who work with brands: UGC creators, YouTubers, Instagram and TikTok creators, and freelance video editors managing several clients at once.

Will it post to my social accounts?

No. The content planner records what you plan and publish; posting happens on your platforms, by you.

Is the feedback check legal advice?

No. It points out where client feedback may go beyond the brief you approved, with the quote it relied on. You decide what counts as a change.

Does it process payments?

No. Payments in CosmoOS are records of the invoices you send, so you can see what is owed. Nothing is charged through it.

How much will it cost?

Pricing is not final yet. A limited free plan is planned alongside paid plans.
